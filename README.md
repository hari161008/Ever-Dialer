# Features of Ever Dialer .✦ ݁˖
<br><br>

### 1. 📞 Core Telephony & Call Management
  1. 📞 **Make & Receive Phone Calls** – Full-featured, reliable default phone dialer and call manager for Android.
  2. 📱 **Multi-SIM Support & Selection** – Dual-SIM support with per-call SIM selection dialog and default SIM preferences.
  3. 📇 **Per-Contact SIM Binding** – Automatically remembers and uses the preferred SIM card for specific contacts.
  4. 🏷️ **SIM Slot Badges in History** – Color-coded SIM 1 and SIM 2 badges displayed directly on call log entries.
  5. 🌐 **Call Accounts Management** – View and switch between active telecom calling accounts and carrier profiles.
  6. ⚡ **Direct Tap-to-Call Mode** – Optional setting to immediately place a call upon tapping any contact or log entry.
  7. 🔁 **Auto Redial** – Automatically retries busy, unanswered, or rejected calls with configurable retry counts and intervals.
  8. 📢 **Auto Speakerphone on Proximity** – Automatically toggles the loudspeaker when the device is moved away from the ear during an active call.
  9. 🛡️ **Pocket Mode Accidental Touch Prevention** – Uses proximity and light sensors to prevent accidental call answering while in a pocket or bag.
  10. ⌨️ **USSD & MMI Code Execution** – Full support for dialing carrier USSD codes, balance checks, and supplementary service codes.
  11. 🚫 **Comprehensive Call Blocking** – Block unwanted callers, spam numbers, private numbers, and unknown callers with a built-in blacklist manager.
  12. 📲 **Default Phone App Integration** – Built-in system prompt and seamless setup workflow to register Ever Dialer as the default phone handler.

---

### 2. 🔢 Smart T9 Dialpad & Dialing Experience
  13. 🔍 **Predictive T9 Search** – Instant contact matching by name, initials, or phone number as you type on the dialpad.
  14. 🧠 **Dialpad Memory** – Automatically preserves the last typed or dialed digits when reopening the dialpad.
  15. ⚡ **One-Tap Speed Dial** – Assign favorite contacts to long-press keys (1–9) for instant speed dialing.
  16. 🔔 **DTMF Audio Key Tones** – Real-time dual-tone multi-frequency keypress feedback during dialing and active calls.
  17. 🎹 **Expressive Dialpad Tone Styles** – Choose between 5 custom dialpad sound profiles: *Standard DTMF*, *Piano*, *Water Drop*, *Mechanical Keyboard*, and *Sci-Fi*.
  18. 📬 **Voicemail Key Shortcut** – Long-press key '1' to quickly dial carrier voicemail.
  19. ➕ **International Prefix Formatting** – Long-press key '0' to insert the '+' international dialing prefix with automated E.164 normalization.
  20. ✋ **One-Handed Dialpad Alignment** – Ergonomically optimized keypad layout for comfortable single-handed operation.
  21. 📋 **Quick Number Copy & Paste** – Long-press dialpad input to quickly paste, copy, or format phone numbers.

---

### 3. 🎛️ Call Controls & Active In-Call Capabilities
  22. 🎛️ **Expressive In-Call Controller** – Modern Material 3 action bar for mute, speaker, bluetooth, dialpad, hold, add call, and call notes.
  23. 🖐️ **Draggable Freeform Button Placement** – Freely drag and reposition every call button anywhere on the screen with persistent coordinates.
  24. 👁️ **Call Button Visibility Controls** – Individually show or hide any of the 10 in-call action buttons to streamline the caller screen.
  25. 📏 **Call Element Sizing Slider** – Dynamically scale call button sizes from 75% to 150% for optimal touch targets.
  26. 🔴 **Hangup Button Customization** – Adjust the width and appearance of the end-call button (compact pill to full-width bar).
  27. 🏷️ **Toggleable Button Labels** – Option to display or hide text labels beneath in-call action buttons for a clean aesthetic.
  28. ⏸️ **Call Hold & Resume** – Place ongoing calls on hold with visual hold indicators and resume anytime.
  29. 🔀 **Conference & Multi-Call Merging** – Manage call waiting, swap active calls, and merge multiple lines into conference calls.
  30. 🎧 **Bluetooth & Audio Routing** – One-tap switching between Earpiece, Speakerphone, Bluetooth Headsets, and Car Audio.
  31. 🔢 **In-Call Keypad Overlay** – Slide-up DTMF keypad during active calls for navigating automated IVR phone trees and menus.

# For those who are wondering how Rate and Review work

1. Create a Google form (with sign in required - to prevent multiple reviews and easily editable)
2. Link the Google form with sheets (Name it as Original form)
3. Create another Google form and name it as Website and link all the columns except the gmail column with the Original form (This is because the original form contains G-mail, which is not supposed to be exposed)
4. Linking is easy, just make the original form to be visible to everyone temperorarly, share the link to AI and ask AI to give the code to link it in the another sheet (This will make the Website form to be automatically updated based on the changes in the original form)
5. It will give you the code, and paste it there
6. Then in the website form, click file > share > publish it on web > publish it as CSV > Publish.
7. Now the Link form the website works a backend raw data
8. If you want to have replies to your website, create a new column in the Website form as replies and type your replies to the respective people who you wanted to.
